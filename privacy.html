Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how rclone-oauth ("we", "our", or "the Application") handles information when you use our service. The Application is designed to facilitate OAuth authentication flows for the open-source software rclone.

2. Data Collection and Usage

We do not collect, store, or share any personal data.

When you authenticate using this Application, the authorization tokens provided by the cloud service provider (e.g., Google, Microsoft) are passed directly back to your local rclone instance. The Application acts solely as a pass-through mechanism to complete the OAuth 2.0 flow.

3. Data Retention

Because the Application does not store any personal data or authentication tokens on any server, there is no data to retain or delete.

4. Third-Party Services

The Application facilitates connections to third-party cloud storage providers. Your interactions with those providers are governed by their respective privacy policies and terms of service.

5. Contact

If you have any questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy, please open an issue on our GitHub repository.
